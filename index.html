OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Vision & Mission

These principles guide the university’s approach to education, innovation, research and partnerships.

Core idea: Empower people, organisations and societies through education, innovative ideas, research and training.

To foster an environment that empowers people, organisations, and societies through education, ideas, research, and training.

  • Provide quality education that fosters social transformation.
  • Create leaders through innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Cultivate a culture of research advancement.
  • Imbibe universal consciousness.
  • Stimulate growth through industrial and international partnerships.
