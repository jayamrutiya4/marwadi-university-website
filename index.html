Why we exist

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To address the challenges facing society and the planet through education that builds the capacity of every learner — nurturing innovative thinking, strong character and a sense of responsibility, so that students use the world's limited resources wisely while meeting the demands of the 21st century.

Our Mission

To deliver industry-aligned, quality education across engineering, management, law, science and health sciences; to promote research, innovation and entrepreneurship; and to build globally competent, socially responsible professionals through hands-on, project-based learning and strong industry collaboration.

Program Specific Outcomes

A snapshot of the specific outcomes defined for select flagship programmes. Select a programme to view its PSOs.

  2. PSO2: Apply the principles of data structures, algorithms, databases and networks to analyse and solve computing problems of varying complexity.
  3. PSO3: Adapt to emerging technologies such as AI, cloud computing and cybersecurity through continuous, self-directed learning for a career in the software industry or research.
  1. PSO1: Apply mechanical engineering fundamentals to design, analyse and manufacture components, machines and thermal systems.
  2. PSO2: Use modern CAD/CAM/CAE tools and automation techniques to solve real-world design and production problems.
  3. PSO3: Function effectively in multidisciplinary teams within manufacturing, automotive and energy industries, with an awareness of sustainability and safety.
  1. PSO1: Analyse business problems using financial, marketing, operations and HR frameworks to support sound managerial decisions.
  2. PSO2: Apply data-driven and analytical tools to evaluate business performance and market opportunities.
  3. PSO3: Demonstrate leadership, ethics and entrepreneurial thinking needed to manage teams and ventures in a global business environment.
  1. PSO1: Apply pharmaceutical sciences to the discovery, formulation, quality control and safe use of drugs.
  2. PSO2: Provide pharmaceutical care through accurate dispensing, patient counselling and awareness of regulatory requirements.
  3. PSO3: Pursue careers in community & hospital pharmacy, pharmaceutical industry, regulatory affairs or research with a commitment to lifelong learning.
